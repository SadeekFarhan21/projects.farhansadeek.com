# UC Berkeley Math 115 Introduction to Number Theory — Problem Sets & Exams

Prof. Kenneth A. Ribet · No stated license (see "Licensing" below)

Source: <https://math.berkeley.edu/~ribet/115/>

Textbook: Niven, Ivan, Herbert S. Zuckerman, and Hugh L. Montgomery. An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers. 5th ed. Wiley, 1991. ISBN: 9780471625469.

Ribet's site keeps four semesters online. Only the assignments still linked from each semester's page survive — the rest were taken down and return 404. Every `hw*.pdf` from 1 to 15 was probed in each directory; what is listed below is everything that exists.

Exam PDFs are the "questions and skeletal solutions" versions, so they carry both the problems and Ribet's worked answers.

## Licensing

Unlike the 18.314 material, which MIT OpenCourseWare publishes under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, these pages carry no license statement — they are a professor's personal course pages. Downloading them for study is ordinary use; re-hosting them under `site/public/` republishes them on putnam-archive.vercel.app, which is a different thing. Worth asking Ribet, or linking to the Berkeley URLs instead of serving copies.
